Our Story

Chocolate as an Art Form

Nourish Bite was born from a simple belief — that chocolate should be more than just sweet. It should nourish, delight, and create memories.

Founded in Lucknow in 2020, we source single-origin cacao from the finest farms in Coorg and Kerala. Every chocolate is hand-tempered in small batches, ensuring perfect snap, shine, and flavour in every piece.
